# Customer Inquiry Management Automation

A business workflow automation built with **Google Forms, Google Sheets, Make.com, and Gmail** to streamline customer inquiry handling for Joelene Enterprise.

## Business Problem

Small businesses may handle customer inquiries manually, making it harder to acknowledge requests promptly and keep track of incoming leads.

## Solution

I built an automated workflow that collects customer inquiries, records submissions in Google Sheets, and uses Make.com to trigger email notifications.

## Workflow
The screenshot shows how Make.com connects Google Forms, Google Sheets, and Gmail to automate customer confirmations and business-owner notifications.
1. A customer submits the online inquiry form.
2. Google Forms records the response in Google Sheets.
3. Make.com watches for new spreadsheet rows.
4. Gmail sends a confirmation email to the customer.
5. Gmail sends a separate notification to the business owner.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Google Forms:** Customer inquiry collection
- **Google Sheets:** Response storage
- **Make.com:** Workflow automation, filters, routing, and error handling
- **Gmail:** Customer confirmations and owner notifications

## Business Value

The workflow reduces repetitive email handling, provides customers with an acknowledgment, and helps the business owner stay informed about incoming inquiries.

## Testing

The workflow was tested using sample customer submissions. Successful tests confirmed that form responses were recorded in Google Sheets and that the email automation worked as expected.
